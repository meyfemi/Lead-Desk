# Krediview: Our Offer & Identity

## 1. Executive Summary
Krediview is Nigeria's trusted business reviews and discovery platform. We connect consumers with credible, verified businesses — and help SMEs build the digital reputation they need to grow. We don't just list businesses; we build the trust infrastructure that turns customer satisfaction into visible, searchable, and permanent social proof.

## 3. Core Service Pillars
- **Business Listings & Verification:** Every business on Krediview goes through a verification process — giving consumers confidence and giving businesses a credibility badge they can display and share.
- **Review Collection & Management:** We provide businesses with the tools to collect genuine customer reviews at the point of experience — via NFC stickers, QR codes, and direct review links.
- **Business Discovery:** Consumers use Krediview to find and vet businesses by category, location, and rating — putting verified SMEs in front of ready-to-spend customers.
- **Reputation Analytics (Dashboard):** Business owners get a real-time view of their ratings, review trends, and customer sentiment through the Business Owner Dashboard.
- **SME Visibility & Growth:** Through category placement, featured listings, and platform-wide campaigns, we help businesses reach more of the right customers — without the ad spend.

## 4. The Krediview Advantage
- **Nigerian-Native:** Built specifically for the Nigerian market — our verification standards, categories, and consumer trust signals are locally relevant, not imported.
- **TRUST. VERIFIED.:** Every listed business carries a verification mark that signals accountability to consumers and credibility to partners.
- **Free to Start:** Businesses can get listed, verified, and begin collecting reviews at no cost — lowering the barrier to entry and letting the value speak first.
- **Owned by the Business:** Reviews belong to the platform, but the reputation belongs to the business. Listings, responses, and profile content are fully controlled by the business owner.
- **Compounding ROI:** Unlike paid ads that stop working when the budget runs out, verified reviews generate trust and inbound enquiries permanently.

## 5. Onboarding Process
1. **Discovery & Listing** — Business is identified, profile created, and submitted for verification.
2. **Verification** — Krediview confirms legitimacy via registration details, address, and contact information.
3. **Activation** — Business receives their NFC/QR review sticker and welcome kit to begin collecting reviews immediately.
4. **First Reviews** — Campaign Officer supports the business in getting their first 5–10 reviews within the first two weeks.
5. **Dashboard Access** — Business owner is onboarded to their dashboard to monitor ratings and respond to reviews.
6. **Optional Growth Features** — Boosted visibility, featured placement, and analytics upgrades available as the relationship develops.
