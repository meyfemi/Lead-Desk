# Target Lead Profile: Krediview Business Onboarding

## 1. Primary Niche
**Established SMEs with Customer-Facing Operations (5–100 employees)**
We are looking for businesses in sectors where trust directly drives purchase decisions: Hospitality & Food, Healthcare & Wellness, Professional Services (Legal, Accounting, Consulting), Education & Training, and Retail.

## 2. Qualifying Criteria (The "Perfect" Lead)
- **Reputation-Dependent Revenue:** They rely on referrals and word-of-mouth. A lack of visible, credible reviews is actively costing them customers.
- **Weak or Absent Local Review Presence:** They may have a few scattered Google or Facebook reviews, but nothing verified or Nigerian-native.
- **Growth-Minded Owner:** They are actively investing in growing the business — promotions, branding, events — but have not yet structured their online reputation.
- **Sufficient Customer Volume:** They handle 10+ customer interactions per week, enough to generate a steady stream of reviews.
- **Geographic Focus:** Lagos first, then Abuja, Port Harcourt, and other major commercial cities where consumers research before spending.

## 3. Decision Maker
- **Titles:** Business Owner, Founder, General Manager, or Marketing Manager.
- **Pain Point:** They have happy customers who say nothing online. New customers Google the business, find no credible signal, and choose a competitor instead. The gap between their actual service quality and their digital visibility is costing them revenue.

## 4. Specific Sales Edges (For Outreach)
- **The "Silence is a Red Flag" Hook:** Business owners assume no reviews means neutral standing. Reframe it: to a new customer, zero reviews is a warning sign. A verified Krediview listing puts trust on display before they even walk in.
- **The "Local Trust Authority" Hook:** Google is global and generic. Facebook is social and informal. Krediview is built specifically for Nigerian consumers — being listed here signals legitimacy to exactly the people deciding whether to patronise the business.
- **The "Free to Start" Hook:** Getting listed and collecting the first reviews costs nothing. Let the value land before any premium conversation begins.
- **The "Referral Engine" Hook:** Every review is organic marketing working around the clock — no ad spend, no effort. A business with 20 verified reviews has 20 pieces of social proof running permanently.
- **The "2-Month Visibility" Hook:** Businesses that onboard and activate their QR/NFC review sticker typically see a meaningful jump in profile visits and inbound enquiries within 8 weeks.

## 5. Red Flags (Disqualify if:)
- Owner reacts defensively to the concept of customer reviews — they will not cooperate with the platform.
- Entirely informal or unregistered operation with no fixed address, phone number, or digital presence — cannot be verified.
- Transient or roadside vendor with no continuity — accountability cannot be established.
- Business currently managing a public PR or legal crisis — first reviews will be negative and not representative.
- Owner has zero digital engagement (no WhatsApp Business, no social media, no email) — activation cost is too high at this stage.
